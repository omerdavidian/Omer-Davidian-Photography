Privacy notice

Updated November 8, 2026

Omer Davidian Photography LLC uses the details you provide through the contact form—your name, email address, optional phone number, photography service, and message—to respond to your inquiry and discuss requested services. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your message.

Service providers

The website is hosted on Vercel. When the online inquiry form is enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile checks for spam, Upstash stores temporary abuse-prevention counters, and Resend delivers your message to our business inbox. These providers process technical information needed to provide their services. The rate limiter uses a keyed hash of your IP address rather than storing the raw address in its counters.

Your information

We do not sell your inquiry details or use the form to subscribe you to marketing. Inquiry correspondence is retained as needed to respond, manage your booking, and meet applicable business obligations. Contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of your inquiry information.

Third-party content

The portfolio includes a Kuula virtual tour. Loading that tour connects your browser to Kuula, which has its own privacy practices. Links to social media and other websites open services with their own policies.
